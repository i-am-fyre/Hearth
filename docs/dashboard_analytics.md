# Dashboard & Analytics

The Hearth dashboard gives you an immediate bird's-eye view of your household's financial health. It aggregates data from all your accounts to help you spot trends and stay on top of your budgets.

## Date Filtering

By default, the dashboard shows data for the **Current Month**. However, the global date picker in the top right corner allows you to filter the entire view.

- You can select presets like **Last 30 Days**, **Year to Date**, or **Last Year**.
- You can also choose **Custom Range** to specify exact start and end dates.
- Changing the date filter will automatically update the **Net Worth Chart**, the **Category Pie Chart**, and the summary totals.

## Net Worth Tracking (Line Chart)

The prominent line chart at the top of the dashboard plots your daily **Net Worth**.

- **Calculation**: Your net worth is dynamically calculated as the sum of all **Asset** accounts minus the sum of all **Liability** accounts for any given day.
- **Interactivity**: You can toggle specific accounts on or off by clicking their names in the chart legend. This allows you to exclude a volatile investment account or focus only on your checking balances.

## Expense Breakdown (Pie Chart)

To the right of the summary metrics is a category breakdown. 

- This chart sums all transactions hitting your **Expense** accounts over the selected date range.
- It groups them by **Account Category** (e.g., Groceries, Rent, Utilities, Entertainment).
- It provides a quick visual indication of where your money is going, making it easier to see if you are overspending in a specific area.

## Account Summaries

Below the charts, Hearth lists all your active accounts grouped by their type:
- **Assets**: Checking, Savings, Cash, Investments.
- **Liabilities**: Credit Cards, Loans, Mortgages.
- **Income**: Salary, Interest, Side Hustles.
- **Expenses**: Groceries, Rent, Utilities.

Each card displays the account's current balance (or total flow for the selected period for Income/Expenses). You can click on any account card to jump directly to its detailed transaction ledger.
