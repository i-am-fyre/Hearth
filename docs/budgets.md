# Budgets

Hearth provides a robust system for planning and tracking your spending through monthly budgets.

## Budget Structure

Budgets in Hearth are organized by **Month** and **Year**. Each budget contains multiple **Budget Lines**.

### Budget Lines
A budget line connects a specific **Category (Account)** to a **Planned Amount**. 

- **Income Categories:** Set your expected earnings for the month.
- **Expense Categories:** Set limits for specific spending areas like Groceries, Rent, or Entertainment.

## Tracking Progress

Once a budget is established, the dashboard provides a real-time comparison between:
1. **Planned Amount:** What you set in your budget.
2. **Actual Spending:** The sum of all transactions categorized to that account during the month.
3. **Remaining:** The difference between planned and actual.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Because Hearth uses Double-Entry accounting, budgets track the flow of money into *Expense* accounts and out of *Income* accounts.
